Academics & Facilities

Why Choose Rishikul Public School?

A comprehensive learning experience with state-of-the-art facilities and dedicated faculty.

Smart Classrooms

Digital learning with projectors, interactive boards, and e-content for an engaging educational experience.

Science Labs

Well-equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs for hands-on practical learning and experiments.

Sports & Fitness

Extensive sports grounds for cricket, football, kabaddi, and indoor games to promote physical health.

Rich Library

A vast collection of books, journals, and digital resources to foster reading habits and knowledge.

Arts & Culture

Music, dance, painting, and cultural programs to nurture creativity and artistic expression.

Transport

Safe and reliable school bus service covering major areas of Jaunpur for students' convenience.

Medical Care

On-campus first-aid facility and regular health check-ups for the wellbeing of every student.

Value Education

Moral and ethical education integrated into curriculum to develop responsible and empathetic citizens.
